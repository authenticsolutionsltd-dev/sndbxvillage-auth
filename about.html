Authentic Booking System

Built by Authentic Solutions Ltd

Authentic Booking System is a room scheduling product built and owned by Authentic Solutions Ltd. Each deployment is configured for a single client organisation. The deployment described on this page is currently configured for SNDBX International LLP (sndbxvillage.com).

What this app does

  • Lets signed-in, authorised team members create, amend, and cancel room bookings
  • Sends booking confirmation, amendment, and cancellation emails to clients
  • Maintains a shared booking log and basic usage reports for the client organisation

Who can use it

This deployment is restricted to a fixed, named list of authorised team members at SNDBX International LLP, who sign in with their own Google account. It is not available to the general public.

What permissions it requests, and why

  • Sign in with Google (email, profile): used only to verify which authorised team member is making or managing a booking. We never request access to Gmail, Drive, or Calendar content.
  • Connect to an external service (UrlFetchApp): used solely to verify a sign-in token directly with Google's own servers, confirming the person signing in is who they say they are.

Data we store

Booking details (date, time, room, client organisation, contact name, and the email address of the team member who made the booking) are stored in a private Google Sheet accessible only to authorised team members of this deployment. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for full details.
