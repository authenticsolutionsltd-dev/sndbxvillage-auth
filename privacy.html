Privacy Policy

Authentic Room Booking · Last updated June 2026

This policy describes how the Authentic Room Booking application ("the app") handles information. The app is a room scheduling product owned and operated by Authentic Solutions Ltd. This deployment of the app is configured for private, internal use by SNDBX International LLP and is not available to the general public.

Who runs this app

This app is built, owned, and maintained by Authentic Solutions Ltd. This particular deployment is operated for SNDBX International LLP's internal use. Questions about this policy can be sent to authenticsolutionsltd@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Sign-in information: when a team member signs in with Google, we receive their name, email address, and a Google-issued token confirming their identity. We do not receive their password.
  • Booking information: details entered when creating a booking — room, date, time, client organisation name, client contact name and email, and number of attendees.

How we use this information

  • To confirm that the person using the app is an authorised team member of this deployment
  • To create, display, and manage room bookings in a shared internal log
  • To send booking confirmation, amendment, and cancellation emails to the relevant client contact
  • To generate basic internal usage reports (e.g. room occupancy, monthly summaries) for the deployment's team

What we don't do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share this information with third parties
  • We do not access Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar, or any other Google service beyond confirming sign-in identity
  • We do not use this information for advertising

Where information is stored

Booking and sign-in information is stored in a private Google Sheet and Google Apps Script project, accessible only to the team members listed as authorised users of this deployment.

Data retention

Booking records are retained as part of SNDBX International LLP's ongoing operational and billing records. Team members can request correction or removal of their own information by contacting bookings@sndbxvillage.com.

Third-party services used

This app uses Google's own infrastructure (Google Sign-In, Google Sheets, Gmail) to operate. Each is governed by Google's own privacy policy. We do not use any analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated date above will reflect the most recent revision.
